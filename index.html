Dashboard development · KPI monitoring

Sales Control Tower

Problem

Sales control lived in a multi-sheet Excel workbook. Every level, from salesman to management, needed its own view of the same targets and thresholds, and the first web version slowed down as data grew.

What I did

Translated the workbook's KPIs, thresholds and escalation logic into a role-based dashboard for five levels: Sales, SPV, ASM, RSM and Management. Led the move from Google Apps Script to a web app on Cloudflare Pages and Supabase, keeping the old version as a fallback during cut-over.

Outcome

One shared view of sales control across the hierarchy, in place of a workbook passed around by hand.

Financial analysis · Receivables

AR Aging & Collection

Problem

Cash-in and open invoices had to be recapped by salesman and area every month and still tie back to the source export. Collection entries arrived through chat and ad-hoc spreadsheets.

What I did

Built a monthly aging recap in five buckets (0–45, 46–75, 76–90, 91–120 and over 120 days) reconciled to source totals, consolidated the outstanding-invoice file, and designed a collection-entry portal that outputs the import format of Accurate Online.

Outcome

Collection follow-up targets the oldest balances by salesman, from a recap that reconciles to the ledger. Scale: about 7,900 customers, 1,580 open invoices and 45 sales staff.

Process improvement · Automation

Reporting Digitalization

Problem

Daily Sales Order and Cash Receipt achievements were posted as free-text WhatsApp messages, and repeated or corrected messages inflated totals. Customer reward claims also moved by chat, with no approval trail.

What I did

Built an input form for ASMs and SPVs with real-time dashboards for six sales areas, enforcing one entry per ASM per day where a correction replaces the earlier entry. Built a four-role reward approval workflow with duplicate-invoice checks, mandatory attachments and an audit trail.

Outcome

Daily figures management can rely on, and promotion rewards that are recorded, checked and auditable.
